PCMB · with NEET coaching

For future doctors and life scientists

PCMB keeps both Biology and Mathematics, so your child can prepare for NEET-UG while keeping engineering and other science courses open through K-CET.

  • Part I: English and a second language
  • Part II: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology
  • Entrance focus: NEET-UG and K-CET
  • Leads to MBBS, BDS, AYUSH, veterinary, pharmacy, nursing, agriculture, B.Sc. and engineering

PCMC · with JEE / K-CET coaching

For future engineers and technologists

PCMC pairs Mathematics with Computer Science, the natural route to engineering through JEE Main and K-CET.

  • Part I: English and a second language
  • Part II: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science
  • Entrance focus: JEE Main and K-CET
  • Leads to B.E./B.Tech., computer science, data science, B.Sc. and B.C.A.

The two years

How the PU years fit together

  1. I PUCFoundations of the board syllabus with entrance-oriented practice from the start.
  2. II PUCKarnataka II PUC board examination (KSEAB), alongside revision for the entrance exams.
  3. EntrancesNEET-UG for medical courses, JEE Main for national engineering institutes, K-CET for Karnataka professional courses.

Ask at counselling for the second-language options, the weekly coaching schedule and the test calendar for 2027–29.

Beyond the syllabus

Support for the whole student

Alongside classes, the college runs soft-skills and life-skills sessions, student counselling and workshops for parents, so families stay involved through the PU years.
