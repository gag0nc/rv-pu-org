Questions parents ask

Frequently asked questions

Which combinations do you offer?

Science: PCMB (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology) and PCMC (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science), each with integrated entrance coaching. For any other stream, please ask the admissions office.

Is NEET, JEE and K-CET coaching part of the programme?

Yes. Entrance coaching for NEET, JEE and K-CET is integrated with the PU course. Ask at counselling how the coaching hours and tests are arranged for your child's combination.

Do you give fee concessions?

Yes. The college offers special concessions based on 10th results. The amount depends on the final marks card. Try the concession check and we will tell you what applies.

What are the fees?

Fees are explained at counselling, together with any concession your child qualifies for. Call 79866 70493 or request a call back.

Is there a hostel or college transport?

Please ask the admissions office about hostel and bus routes from your area; they will give you the current details.

Which boards can apply?

Students who have passed Class 10 from the Karnataka State board, CBSE, ICSE or another recognised board can apply.

Can we visit the campus before deciding?

Yes, please do. Book a campus visit and we will call to fix a time.
